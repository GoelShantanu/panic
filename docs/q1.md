## 1. The Question

> **Do we accept re-basing the primary segment from F&O day-traders/scalpers to serious swing/positional investors?**

Restated as the decision actually being made: **whose needs break ties?**

When a design choice pits speed against calm, density against clarity, or engagement against restraint — which user wins? That is the whole question. Everyone else can still use the product; they just don't get to decide arguments.

## 2. Two Research Corrections Raised While Preparing This

Both surfaced during preparation on 2026-07-15 and both bear directly on the decision. `docs/research/phase-01-product-research.md` is approved and immutable, so these require **errata** per [GUARDRAILS.md](../GUARDRAILS.md) §1.2 — flagged here, not edited.

### ⚠ Correction 1 — the RA/IA count is wrong by ~4×, against the recommendation

`[VERIFIED]` Phase 1 §3.3 states: *"SEBI-registered RAs & IAs — ~10k+ entities — High willingness to pay (₹2k–10k/mo)."*

Actual:

| Category | Count | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| SEBI-registered Investment Advisers | **~988** | [Univest](https://univest.in/blogs/sebi-registered-stock-advisor) |
| SEBI-registered Research Analysts | **~1,330** (Aug 2024) – **1,500+** | [Trade Brains](https://tradebrains.in/no-of-sebi-registered-research-analysts-and-investment-advisors/), [AlgoTest](https://algotest.in/tools/research-analysts) |
| **Combined** | **~2,500 — not 10,000+** | |

**Impact:** at ₹5k/mo with 100% market share the ceiling is **≈₹15 crore/year**, not the ₹60 cr+ implied. This weakens Option C below and partially undercuts OQ-5's B2B thesis. The error worked *against* the option this brief recommends, which is worth noting for calibration.

### ⚠ Correction 2 — the "nobody will pay" objection is answered, and not by us

`[VERIFIED]` Phase 1 §7.2 warned: *"Zerodha's Pulse sets the retail price anchor at ₹0."*

**Moneycontrol Pro crossed 1 million paying subscribers** (announced Oct 2024) — India's largest media subscription product, **among the top 15 globally**, with subscriber numbers close to the Financial Times and Barron's. ([Forbes India](https://www.forbesindia.com/article/news/network18s-moneycontrol-pro-crosses-1-million-paying-subscribers-indias-largest-digital-subscription-platform-now-among-top-15-globally/94461/1), [Storyboard18](https://www.storyboard18.com/brand-marketing/network18s-moneycontrol-pro-crosses-1-million-paying-subscribers-indias-largest-digital-subscription-platform-now-among-top-15-globally-45694.htm))

**Impact, both directions:**
- ✅ Indian retail investors demonstrably pay for financial information **at scale**. The free-anchor objection is empirically false.
- ⚠️ The incumbent already owns a **payment relationship with 1M of our target users**. Phase 1 §7.2's read that Moneycontrol's publisher conflict is a permanent structural weakness still holds — but they are not merely a publisher; they are the largest subscription business in Indian media.

## 3. The Population Funnel — Now Sourced

`[VERIFIED]`

| Layer | Count | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| NSE trading accounts (UCCs) | **26 crore** | Business Standard, Jun 2026 |
| Unique registered investors (PAN) | **13.1 crore** | Outlook Money, May 2026 |
| **Active clients — placed ≥1 trade in 12 months** | **4.42 crore** | [StartupTalky](https://startuptalky.com/india-stock-broking-market-june-2026-analysis/), Jun 2026 |
| F&O individual traders | **42.7 lakh** ↓ from 61.4 lakh (Q1→Q4 FY25) | SEBI FY25 study |
| SEBI RAs + IAs | **~2,500** | §2 above |

**Reading the funnel:**

- **Two-thirds of registered investors do not trade in a year.** 13.1 cr registered → 4.42 cr active.
- **F&O scalpers are ~10% of active clients** (42.7L / 4.42cr ≈ 9.7%) — and that 10% contracted ~30% in three quarters.
- **Non-derivative active base ≈ 40 million accounts.** `[INFERRED]` At the ~2:1 account:person ratio observed at the top of the funnel, that is **≈25–30 million unique people**. `[ASSUMPTION]` — the ratio is measured at the registered-investor level (26cr:13.1cr) and assumed to hold for active clients; it is not independently sourced.

**Calibration note:** Phase 1 §3.3 estimated this segment at "1–2 crore" by feel. The sourced figure is larger. The original estimate was directionally right for the wrong reason.

## 4. Why This Decision Matters

**It is the highest-fan-out decision in the project.** It does not set a parameter; it sets the **objective function** against which every later trade-off is resolved.

| Downstream | How OQ-1 determines it |
| --- | --- |
| **Default view** (frontend) | Scalper → live-tail stream. Swing → *diff since last visit*. Different information architectures — Phase 1 §5.2 establishes a density toggle cannot turn a stream into a diff. |
| **Notification model** | Scalper → high-volume, low-latency. Swing → low-volume, holdings-scoped; restraint as the premium feature (§4.6). |
| **Latency budget** (architecture, infra cost) | Scalper → sub-second *is* the product; sustained infra spend. Swing → seconds acceptable; latency is table stakes (§4.5). |
| **Retention hook** | Scalper → feed speed. Swing → watchlist + accumulated story history (§5.3). Different first builds. |
| **Roadmap** (OQ-3, OQ-5) | Scalper → desktop terminal. Swing → mobile digest viable, possibly primary. |
| **Success metrics** | Scalper → sessions/day. Swing → renewal rate. **These cannot both be optimised.** |

### ⚠ Direct collision with an approved guardrail

[GUARDRAILS.md](../GUARDRAILS.md) §4.10: *"No engagement mechanics that measurably increase trading frequency in a 91%-loss cohort… This is a red line, not a tunable."*

If scalpers become the primary user, **the product's core job is to increase their trading activity.** Option A therefore requires either amending §4.10 or accepting an incoherent rulebook. This is a real cost of Option A, not a rhetorical device.

## 5. Options

### Option A — Scalper-first *(the original brief)*

Optimise for the F&O day-trader. Terminal Mode default, sub-second stream, maximum density.

| Dimension | Assessment |
| --- | --- |
| **Advantages** | Loudest, most engaged users. Fastest word-of-mouth in a tight community. Engagement metrics that fundraise well. Unambiguous product definition — speed. |
| **Disadvantages** | 42.7 lakh and **falling ~30%/yr**. 91% lose money → structural annual churn. SEBI is *actively* shrinking the cohort; this is policy, not weather. Permanent CAC treadmill. |
| **Technical impact** | Highest bar: sub-second ingest→render, WebSocket fan-out, aggressive infra. |
| **Product impact** | Narrow and sharp. Easiest to build and explain. |
| **Business impact** | Catastrophic LTV — users get liquidated and leave. TAM shrinking by regulatory design. |
| **Cost** | **Highest** — infra + perpetual CAC + regulatory legal exposure. |
| **Complexity** | Medium technically; **high politically**. |
| **Risk** | 🔴 **Existential.** Requires amending GUARDRAILS §4.10. Optimises a funnel pointed at the regulator's crosshairs. |

### Option B — Swing/positional-first *(recommended)*

Optimise for the serious multi-year investor with real capital. Scalpers welcome; they don't break ties.

| Dimension | Assessment |
| --- | --- |
| **Advantages** | ≈25–30M unique people (sourced). They **renew**. Moneycontrol Pro proves 1M+ already pay. No rule amendments needed. Regulatory tailwind, not headwind. |
| **Disadvantages** | Lower urgency → weaker viral loop, slower initial growth. *"Nothing happened today"* is hard to demo. Head-on against Moneycontrol Pro's existing payment relationship. |
| **Technical impact** | Moderate. Latency is table stakes; real work moves to **dedup + entity resolution + filings** — Phase 1 §2.1's identified moat. **Engineering effort points at the defensible thing.** |
| **Product impact** | Harder. Requires the *diff* model. Comfortable Mode becomes a distinct IA, not a padding setting. |
| **Business impact** | Best LTV. Renewal-driven. ₹199–499/mo plausible — **exact figure still unsourced**. |
| **Cost** | Moderate infra, moderate CAC, higher product-design cost. |
| **Complexity** | Higher product complexity, lower infra complexity. |
| **Risk** | 🟡 Medium. Principal risk: we build a calm product nobody feels urgency to open. |

### Option C — Professional-first (RAs / IAs / HNIs)

Target the ~2,500 registered professionals with compliance monitoring obligations.

| Dimension | Assessment |
| --- | --- |
| **Advantages** | Budget lines exist. Compliance becomes a *feature*. Extremely sticky. Classic Christensen beachhead — start narrow, expand down. |
| **Disadvantages** | **~2,500 entities.** ≈₹15 cr/yr ceiling at ₹5k/mo and 100% share. Sales-led motion. |
| **Technical impact** | Lowest bar — filings-first, no consumer polish, no scale problem. |
| **Product impact** | A compliance tool, not a news product. |
| **Business impact** | Real revenue fast; **ceiling is a rounding error** against 25–30M consumers. |
| **Cost** | Low infra, **high sales cost per rupee**. |
| **Complexity** | Low. |
| **Risk** | 🟠 High **strategic** risk — building the wrong company. §2's 4× error made this look better than it is. |

### Option D — B2B API-first

Sell clean, tagged, deduplicated news to brokers and fintechs. Retail app is a demo, or absent.

| Dimension | Assessment |
| --- | --- |
| **Advantages** | Highest margin, lowest CAC, best churn. Smallest MVP — **no consumer UI**. Revenue soonest. |
| **Disadvantages** | No crowd → **no data flywheel**, no brand, no consumer defensibility. stockinsights.ai already occupies this lane with AI-tagged BSE/NSE filings. Sales-led company. |
| **Technical impact** | Pipeline only, no frontend. Substantially less work. |
| **Product impact** | Infrastructure, not product. Everything learned from the CryptoPanic study — crowd labelling, triage UX, keyboard model — becomes irrelevant. |
| **Business impact** | Good margins, **but you become a vendor, not a platform.** Customers can rebuild it once they see it works. |
| **Cost** | Lowest build, highest sales. |
| **Complexity** | Low–medium. |
| **Risk** | 🟠 High — a different company than the vision describes. **Pre-empts OQ-5.** |

### Option E — Dual-track: swing-first consumer + parallel B2B API

Both B and D, simultaneously.

| Dimension | Assessment |
| --- | --- |
| **Advantages** | One pipeline serves both — the API is a thin layer over what B needs anyway. B2B revenue subsidises consumer CAC. Hedged. |
| **Disadvantages** | **Two products, two motions, one founder.** Product-led and sales-led are different companies. Classic pre-PMF failure mode. |
| **Technical impact** | Shared pipeline; two surfaces + public API contract, versioning, SLAs, support. |
| **Product impact** | Divided attention exactly when focus matters most. |
| **Business impact** | Best case strong; realistic case neither reaches escape velocity. |
| **Cost** | **Highest total.** |
| **Complexity** | 🔴 Highest. |
| **Risk** | 🟠 High — and **pre-empts OQ-5**, which exists to decide this separately. |

## 6. Recommendation

### **Option B — Swing/positional-first.**

**The new evidence moved the board toward B independently of the prior view.** The strongest counter to B in Phase 1 was *"Pulse is free, nobody pays."* Moneycontrol Pro's 1M+ paying subscribers — top 15 globally, FT-comparable — refutes that with a sourced number. Simultaneously, the RA/IA count collapsing from 10k to 2,500 removes the most credible alternative beachhead. **Both corrections arrived from research, not from advocacy.**

**The segment is ~60× larger and moving the opposite way.** ≈25–30M unique active non-derivative investors versus 42.7 lakh scalpers shrinking 30%/yr.

**It points engineering at the moat.** Option A spends the budget on latency, which Phase 1 §4.5 establishes is rentable by any funded competitor. Option B spends it on dedup and entity resolution — which compound, and which Pulse cannot follow without building our pipeline. **B is the option where the hard work and the defensible work are the same work.**

**It is the only option requiring no amendments.** A needs GUARDRAILS §4.10 rewritten. D and E pre-empt OQ-5. C requires accepting a ₹15 cr ceiling.

### The honest cost of B

**B commits us to a harder product problem.** A live-tail stream is a solved design; a *"what changed since you last looked"* diff is not — and Phase 1 §5.2 argues it cannot be reached by tuning a stream. **Choosing B makes Phase 3 genuinely novel design work rather than a CryptoPanic port.** The reverse-engineering study becomes a source of *patterns*, not a template.

If the priority is the fast, known path, that is a real argument for A and it should not be dismissed.

### What would change this recommendation

Evidence that swing investors won't pay **for news specifically**. Moneycontrol Pro bundles research, analysis, technical charts, and CNBC streaming — their 1M may be paying for something considerably broader than what we would offer. **This is the weakest link in the case for B and it remains unsourced.**

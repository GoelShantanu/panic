## 1. The Question — As It Stands After D-011

The question as originally written covered **all** sentiment. D-011/D-012 settled the user half: user directional votes may aggregate to a security, labelled as user opinion. What remains is the machine half:

> **Does StockPanic's AI say anything about tone or direction, and if so, where may that judgement appear?**

Three sub-questions, answered together:

1. **Do we generate AI tone at all?**
2. **If so, what does it attach to:** the article, or the security?
3. **How is the boundary enforced:** schema, service separation, or policy?

---

## 2. Two Corrections Raised While Preparing This

Both bear directly on the options. `phase-01-product-research.md` is approved and immutable, so these are **flagged, not edited** (GUARDRAILS §1.2).

### 2.1 "Structurally unrepresentable" is unachievable as Research specified it — candidate erratum E-6

Research §6.2 and E3 require that no schema path aggregate tone to a security. `docs/q2.md` §F.2 already retracted this: if `article.tone` exists and `article ↔ instrument` tags exist, then **tone per security is one JOIN away**, whatever the schema says. The research claim is a target no relational design can meet while both facts live in one database.

**The achievable forms are:** (a) don't store tone at all; (b) store it somewhere that cannot see instrument tags (physical separation, `docs/q2.md` §F.3); or (c) store it alongside and rely on API surface, CI tests and review. Only (a) is "unrepresentable" in the literal sense.

### 2.2 CryptoPanic parity does not include AI sentiment

`[INFERRED]` from the RE study: the reference product's sentiment layer is **entirely crowd-generated** (Bullish/Bearish filters come from votes, `[RE §4.4]`, RE line ~514). Its only AI surface found is a **per-user AI summary** (`/web-api/user/last_ai_summary`, `[RE §3]` `[VERIFIED]` endpoint, behaviour `[ASSUMPTION]`). Its proprietary computed signal, the **Panic Score**, measures **news activity against a trailing baseline**, not direction `[RE §5.2]`.

*Caveat:* the study covers public artefacts only. The logged-in product was not examined (PROJECT_STATE B-3), so an AI-tone feature behind login cannot be ruled out.

**Implication:** "do what CryptoPanic does" (D-011) points *away* from AI tone, not towards it.

## 3. Why This Decision Matters

### 3.1 It decides whose voice the directional signal is in

D-012 made user-vote aggregation permissible on one condition: it is **labelled as user opinion, never as the product's assessment**. That framing is what the IT Act §79 intermediary argument rests on (`docs/q2.md` §F.2): we host speech, we don't make it.

`[INFERRED]` **An AI tone label is the product's assessment, by definition.** Put "AI: positive" beside "Bullish (412) · Bearish (88)" and the page now carries *our* directional judgement next to the crowd's, and the reader cannot easily separate the two. AI tone does not just add its own exposure: **it weakens the defence D-011 depends on.**

### 3.2 Mis-tone is the same trust event as mis-tag

GUARDRAILS §4.6 treats a mis-tagged instrument as a trust-extinction event. `[ASSUMPTION]` A mis-toned article is the same failure. Indian financial text is hard for sentiment models: results "below estimates but up YoY", promoter pledge *release* versus *creation*, mixed English/Hindi headlines. A confidently wrong "positive" on a filing that markets read as negative is visible, screenshot-able, and on a regulated security.

### 3.3 It sets the AI layer's scope before counsel is engaged

GUARDRAILS §3.5 gates AI-layer implementation on counsel (OQ-8), and §4.13 now gates directional voting on counsel. Whatever is chosen here becomes part of the brief to counsel. A smaller AI surface is a cheaper, faster legal opinion.

### 3.4 What depends on it

| Downstream | Dependency |
| --- | --- |
| **Schema** | Whether a `tone` attribute exists anywhere, and where. |
| **AI pipeline** | Whether we build, evaluate and monitor a sentiment model, which is a standing accuracy commitment, not a one-off build. |
| **B2B (OQ-5)** | `[INFERRED]` AI tone in the partner feed is a directional signal from *us*, which carries the same intermediary-association risk as `docs/q2.md` §2.2, but worse, because it is unambiguously our voice. |
| **Counsel scope (OQ-8)** | Every directional surface adds a question. |
| **Competition** | `[VERIFIED, Research §7.2]` stockinsights.ai ships AI sentiment tags on BSE/NSE filings today. |

## 4. Options

All options share one non-negotiable: **AI summaries are attributed and tone-neutral** (Research §6.3, §8 item 3; GUARDRAILS §4.5). A summary that says "strong results" is a tone label by another route.

### Option A — AI sentiment per security *(stockinsights.ai-style)*

AI produces a per-security reading ("TATAMOTORS: positive news flow today"), displayable and rankable.

| Dimension | Assessment |
| --- | --- |
| **Advantages** | Feels intelligent. Matches a live competitor. A sellable data product. |
| **Disadvantages** | Exactly Research §6.2 Landmine 1. Our voice, on a security, in real time. Undercuts D-011's framing (§3.1). |
| **Technical** | Sentiment model + aggregation + calibration + monitoring. |
| **Business** | 🔴 Likely forecloses broker/B2B distribution (§3.4). |
| **Risk** | 🔴 **Severe.** Requires a further GUARDRAILS §4.3/§4.4 amendment. |

### Option B — AI tone on articles only, displayed, never aggregated *(GUARDRAILS §4.3 as amended)*

Each article carries an AI badge: "This article's tone: positive / negative / neutral." No per-security roll-up, no ranking.

| Dimension | Assessment |
| --- | --- |
| **Advantages** | The Research §6.2 reframing: an article attribute, not a security attribute. Useful for scanning. |
| **Disadvantages** | §3.1: on a page that already shows per-security crowd sentiment, an AI tone badge reads as the platform's opinion. The JOIN still exists (§2.1), so enforcement is separation and review, not schema. |
| **Technical** | Sentiment model, plus separation per `docs/q2.md` §F.3, plus CI tests. |
| **Business** | Must be excluded from the partner API to protect OQ-5. |
| **Cost** | Medium, and continuing: model evaluation on Indian financial text. |
| **Risk** | 🟠 Medium. Defensible in isolation; weaker beside D-011. |

### Option C — No AI tone. AI does factual classification and neutral summaries only

AI tags **what happened**, not how it reads: event type (results, order win, block deal, rating action, promoter pledge change, board meeting, litigation), materiality category, and an attributed, tone-neutral summary. **No `tone` attribute exists anywhere.** The only directional signal on the platform is user votes (D-011).

| Dimension | Assessment |
| --- | --- |
| **Advantages** | **Literally unrepresentable**, the only option that meets the original OQ-4 intent (§2.1a). Keeps D-011's "user speech, not our voice" framing clean. **CryptoPanic parity** (§2.2). Event types are facts and are verifiable against the filing. Event-type filters ("show me only results and order wins") serve D-009's promise directly. Smallest counsel question. |
| **Disadvantages** | No AI tone feature; stockinsights.ai has one. Users wanting "is this good or bad?" get the crowd's answer, not ours. |
| **Technical** | Classification model, which is easier to evaluate than tone because it has ground truth (the filing type and its content). No sentiment model. |
| **Business** | Keeps the partner feed clean. Event-tagged filings are a credible B2B product in their own right. |
| **Cost** | Lowest. No separation service needed for AI tone. |
| **Risk** | 🟢 Low. Main risk: perceived as less "AI-powered". |

### Option D — AI tone computed internally, never displayed

Tone is generated for internal use only (e.g. dedup clustering, quality signals), never shown or exposed.

| Dimension | Assessment |
| --- | --- |
| **Advantages** | Keeps a capability without a public surface. |
| **Disadvantages** | GUARDRAILS §4.4 already bars ranking by it, so its uses are thin. It is still stored (the §2.1 JOIN), and still discoverable by a regulator or a leak. All of B's model cost with almost none of the benefit. |
| **Risk** | 🟠 Medium. Same shape as `docs/q2.md` Option C, "worst of both". |

### Option E — C now, B after counsel

Ship C. Put B to counsel alongside §4.13's voting question; add B later only if counsel and the product case both support it.

| Dimension | Assessment |
| --- | --- |
| **Advantages** | C's benefits now; B kept as a real option, not a lost one. |
| **Disadvantages** | `[INFERRED]` Unlike deferring voting, adding tone later is **cheap**: a new attribute on articles, behind separation. So deferral costs little here. The asymmetry favours waiting. |
| **Risk** | 🟢 Low. |

---

## 5. Recommendation

### **Option E — no AI tone at MVP (Option C); revisit article-level tone (Option B) after counsel.**

## 6. Why

**It protects the decision you've already made.** D-011 made directional sentiment a headline feature. Its defensibility rests on that sentiment being *users'* speech. AI tone is the one addition that would make the platform a speaker too, on the same screen. Keeping the AI out of direction is how the voting feature stays defensible.

**It is what CryptoPanic actually does.** The crowd supplies sentiment; the machine supplies activity (Panic Score) and summaries. Option C mirrors that split.

**It is the only option where the rule is enforced by absence.** No tone column means no JOIN, no separation service, no CI test to maintain, and nothing for a future engineer to misuse. GUARDRAILS §4.3 becomes trivially true.

**Event types are more useful than tone for D-009's promise.** "Never miss what matters" is served by *what kind of thing happened*: results, order wins, pledge changes. "Positive tone" is a weaker filter and an unverifiable one. Classification has ground truth; tone mostly doesn't.

**Deferral is cheap this time.** Adding article tone later is additive, so waiting for counsel costs little.

### The honest cost of E

- **We cede an AI feature to stockinsights.ai.** If the market rewards "AI says positive", we will lag at launch.
- **"AI-powered" becomes harder to market.** Classification and neutral summaries are less flashy than sentiment.
- **The crowd becomes the only directional voice.** At launch, with few voters, most stories will show "Be the first to vote" (`docs/q2.md` §F.4.2), so for early users there is effectively *no* directional signal.

### What would change this recommendation

- Counsel advises that article-level AI tone, clearly labelled, is outside the IA Regulations perimeter even alongside crowd voting → move to B.
- Research on stockinsights.ai shows they hold a counsel opinion or regulatory comfort → reopen B sooner.
- User research shows tone is a top-3 reason to choose a product → weigh B against the risk explicitly.

### Adjacent, not part of OQ-4

A **Panic Score equivalent**, meaning news activity per instrument against its own trailing baseline, is a **volume fact**, permitted by GUARDRAILS §4.4 ("most discussed"). It is the CryptoPanic feature most compatible with the rules and is worth considering in the PRD. It needs §4.11's market-session awareness, because activity baselines differ in and out of trading hours.
